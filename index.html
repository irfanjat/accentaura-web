About AccentAura

Acoustic machine learning system developed for university educational exhibitions.

Project Purpose

AccentAura was designed for the University English Language and Literature Department exhibition to demonstrate phonological diversity and acoustic ear training. Visitors can speak into the terminal and scientifically observe how regional phonetic markers shape spoken English.

Acoustic Technologies

Built using traditional audio signal processing and machine learning:

  • Feature Extraction: 188 dimensions incorporating 20 MFCCs, Delta dynamics, spectral roll-off, spectral contrast, and chroma STFT.
  • Machine Learning: Supervised classification evaluated via Stratified Group K-Fold cross-validation split by speaker.
  • Zero Generative AI: Operates entirely on raw sound waveforms without speech-to-text transcription or cloud AI models.

Dataset Provenance

Training models were prepared with real human recordings from the Speech Accent Archive (George Mason University), made available under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-NC-SA 4.0) license.

Visitor Privacy

All microphone recordings are processed locally for acoustic analysis. Recordings are temporary and automatically cleared when a session resets, ensuring full visitor privacy.
